Moving Meshes#

Degrees of freedom in this library live on the fixed reference element, so a space map that moves in time does not change the basis. It changes the mass matrix and the operators built from it, and those changes enter a time-dependent problem in exactly two places.

The Lie derivative#

For a map \(F(\xi, t)\) with mesh velocity \(w = \partial_t F\), the pullback identity for time-dependent diffeomorphisms is

\[\partial_t (F_t^* \omega) = F_t^* \big(\partial_t \omega + \mathcal{L}_w \omega\big), \qquad \mathcal{L}_w = \mathrm{d}\,\iota_w + \iota_w\,\mathrm{d}.\]

The mesh motion therefore contributes \(\mathcal{L}_w\) on every \(k\)-form field of the state, plus the mass evaluated per stage as \(M(t)\). No \(\dot M\) is ever assembled: under the marcher contract \(M(t)\dot y = r(y, t)\) the change of the volume element is carried entirely by the stage values of \(M\).

For a form of the ambient dimension the exterior derivative vanishes, so \(\mathcal{L}_w\) reduces to advection by the relative velocity \(v - w\). That makes the discrete geometric conservation law structural: at \(v = w\) the advection and Lie derivative assemblies are the same discrete operator and cancel exactly, so a density carried along by the mesh does not move in the reference frame to better than the solver tolerance.

fdg.lie_derivative_operator(smap: SpaceMap, specs: KFormSpecs, velocity_points: NDArray[float64]) → NDArray[float64][source]#

Assemble the Lie derivative of a form with respect to a mesh velocity.

This is \(\mathcal{L}_w = \mathrm{d}\,\iota_w + \iota_w\,\mathrm{d}\) for any form order. Each term puts the exterior derivative on the side of the strong-conversion mass matching its own form order.

Prescribed motion#

Time dependence is specified as geometry degrees of freedom sampled at the collocation nodes of every slab, which makes the mesh velocity a spectral derivative of that interpolant rather than a finite difference. The same tableau that integrates the state differentiates the geometry, so the two are treated consistently. The velocity reaches the interior product as unscaled physical components, which is that function’s convention; the map applies its own metric factors.

Fluid-structure coupling#

The fluid-structure pattern reuses the same primitives with the geometry as part of the state: append the geometry degrees of freedom to the marched vector, and have the residual rebuild the maps of the current iterate with space_maps_from_geometry_dofs(). The resulting joint system is autonomous in time, so the quadratic invariants of the coupled problem are conserved exactly by the Gauss collocation scheme rather than to the truncation error of the timestep.

MovingMesh.mass_factory() returns the matrices block diagonal over the elements; the hybridized continuity constraints of Mesh Topology compose with them but are not applied by this module.
